About Wyeth Roofing

Built above.
Built to last.

30+ years of combined roofing experience across the leadership team.

Jack Wyeth & Jack Jenden — Directors

Wyeth Roofing was established in 2023 by Jack Wyeth, who saw the need for a roofing company that put quality workmanship, reliability and customer satisfaction at the heart of its service.

Since then the business has grown steadily, building a strong reputation for dependable roofing solutions and consistently high standards on every project.

In 2026, Jack Jenden joined Wyeth Roofing as a Director, bringing further experience and expertise to the business. Together, Jack Wyeth and Jack Jenden form the leadership team — more than 30 years of combined industry experience and a shared commitment to a professional, straightforward and reliable service.
